# ADR 0001: Scope of the portfolio

Status: Accepted (2026-09-28)

## Decision
Build a single-page portfolio with anchor-link navigation and no subpages.

## Why
Recruiters skim. One page with clear sections is fastest to read and simplest
to host and maintain.

## Considered and rejected
- Multi-page site: more navigation, more to maintain, no benefit for a first version.
- Contact form: needs a backend email service, API keys, and spam protection.
  Contact is email and LinkedIn links instead.
- Blog or CMS: large to build and test; not needed to show her work.

# ADR 0002: Content source and privacy

Status: Accepted (2026-09-28)

## Decision
All content comes from Stephanie's current honest resume and her verified
project code. The public page shows email and LinkedIn only.

## Why
- The portfolio must match her resume and LinkedIn; any mismatch hurts trust.
- Phone number and full address stay off a public page for privacy.

## Rules
- Projects list only methods and datasets confirmed in her code or reports.
- Tableau Public link is added only once she provides the real URL.

## Considered and rejected
- Adding placeholder metrics or projects to "fill out" the page: rejected; not honest.
